Hunza Valley

Introduction

Hunza Valley is one of the most beautiful tourist places in Pakistan. It is located in Gilgit-Baltistan and is famous for its mountains, rivers, natural beauty, and friendly people.

History

Hunza was once an independent princely state. In 1974, it became part of Pakistan. The valley has a rich history and was an important stop on the ancient Silk Road.

Attractions

  • Altit Fort
  • Baltit Fort
  • Attabad Lake
  • Passu Cones
  • Khunjerab Pass

Travel Steps

  1. Start your journey from Islamabad.
  2. Travel via the Karakoram Highway.
  3. Reach Gilgit.
  4. Continue to Hunza Valley.
  5. Visit the famous tourist attractions.
